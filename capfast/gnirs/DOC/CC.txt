The components controller is composed of two levels.

The lower level can be controlled completely by the command line, or through an
epics interface.  The command line interface was meant to be a development and
debugging tool.  All scientific work will be done through the EPICS interface.
The EPICS interface can control the CC with use of the CC Engineering screens. 
In a working system, however, there will be two levels above it.  The Instrument
Sequencer and the OCS.
