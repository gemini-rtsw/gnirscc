4.4.7

The function that opens the serial port to the temperature control card is in
openprt2.c.  All of the other functions related to reading the temperature card
are in epicsDev.c.  init_aiTempPort, init_aoTempPort, read_aiTempPort and 
write_aoTempPort are the routines for the epics temperature card device driver.  
The other routines in the file are for converting from a voltage to the actual
units of the record.  Most of these are called from the subroutine records.  When
the ai or ao is fired, it gets the current value and passes the value to the 
subroutine record for further processing.  When done, the value is placed in an 
epics record.
