All of the bc635VME related routines are located in bc350time.c.  It includes
 
	reading the time from the network (getServerTime)
	setting the time on the bc635VME card (initTime)
	getting the current time (getTime)
	getting the universal time (getUTime).  This is only valid
		if the local time is loaded.  
	printing the current time (printTime)
