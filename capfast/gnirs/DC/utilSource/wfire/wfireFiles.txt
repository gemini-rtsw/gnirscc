This area has two files, putWFMsg.c and WFireMessageUtil.c.  They are part of 
the wfire message device driver.  putWFMsg.c has the routines that read and write
 the message to the transputers in wfire message format.  The WFireMessageUtil.c 
file contains the tasks that deal with the queues that sends messages to EPICS 
and receives messages from epics and the transputers.  These tasks communicate
to the epics driver in epicstp through two queues, WFMsg_Q_ID_in and 
WFMsg_Q_ID_out.  The other queues are used to communicate between tasks in the
WFireMessageUtil.c file.
