This area has the actual routines that get run when one of the epics records
with wFireVarMsg or wFireCmdMsg are fired.  The routine related to that record
(ai,ao,li,lo,si,so) is then run.  The queue structure is setup and placed on
the WFMsg_Q_ID_out queue.  The response is returned in the WFMsg_Q_ID_in queue.
Any messages other than the response are taken care of in one of the tasks in the
wfire area.
