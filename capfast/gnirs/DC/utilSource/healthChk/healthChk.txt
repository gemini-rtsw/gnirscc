2.1.?  healthChk

The healthcheck routines are a way to set a general health condition of the system.
There are 8 seperate areas that are checked.  These are:
	voltage
	preAmp
	wfire
	power supply
	temperature
	adc
	datacube
	gfci

There are several voltages that are checked for each of these except datacube and
wfire.
	
   Checked Voltages

	voltage health - vddcl1, vddcl2, vggcl1, vggcl2, vdet, pr/cd VRowOff, 
	pr/cd VRstOn, pr/cd VP,  
c64_79:a2, 
	pr/cd BOK, p/a 4 VOff1, pr/cd VnRow,
	pr/cd VnCol, pr/cd VddUC, pr/cd VRowOn, pr/cd VRstOff, pr/cd VddOut,
	p/a 1 VOff 2,	p/a 2 VOff 2, p/a 2 VOff 1, p/a 3 VOff 2, 
	p/a 3 VOff 1

	preAmp health - combination of adc and power supply healths

	power supply health - p/a 1 VOff1, p/a 1 +5 Sup, p/a 1 +5 Ref, p/a 2 -5 Ref,
		p/a 2 +5 Ref, p/a 3 -5 Ref, p/a 3 +5 Ref, p/a 4 VOff 2, p/a 4 -5 Ref,
		p/a 4 +5 Sup, 	p/a 4 +5 Ref, DRcvr Vcc 1, pr/cd VdsLR, pr/cd -7 Ref,

		pr/cd -15 Sup, p/a hk -15 sup, p/a hk -15 sup,  pr/cd +5 Sup,
